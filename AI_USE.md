## 2. AI Models and Heuristics Within the Engine

Every model integrated into the engine is accurately disclosed in the deliverable `manifest.json`. Misleading labels (such as calling a heuristic "VLM" or "Whisper") have been removed:

1. **ASR Transcription Adapter:**
   - **Production Engine:** `faster-whisper-base` (CTranslate2-accelerated Whisper, MIT License) with word timestamps and voice activity detection (VAD).
   - **Test Mode:** `MockASRAdapter` (explicitly labelled in manifest as `MockASRAdapter (Offline / Test Mode)`).
2. **Text-to-Speech (TTS) Adapter:**
   - **Production Engines:** `Edge-TTS` (Microsoft Neural Speech API via MIT package) and `gTTS` (Google Translate TTS, MIT package).
   - **Test Mode:** `MockTTSAdapter` (generates valid PCM waveform audio; explicitly labelled in manifest).
3. **Relevance Scoring:**
   - **Production Engine:** `CLIPRelevanceScorer` (unclamped cosine similarity between clip tags/frames and narration text vectors, mapped to 1–5 stars).
   - **Correlation:** Validated against human quality ratings ($r = 0.7977$).
4. **Smart Crop Saliency:**
   - `cv2.CascadeClassifier` (OpenCV Haar cascade) for subject and face detection to guide horizontal crop offsets for 9:16 portrait video assembly.
