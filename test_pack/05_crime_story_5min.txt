Rain falls steadily over the cobblestone alleys of the historic banking district. Under the cover of a fierce thunderstorm, shadowy figures disable the high-voltage perimeter sensors of the central vault. Inside the subterranean corridor, laser tripwires crisscross the cold marble floor. The master locksmith bypasses the heavy titanium tumblers with millimeter precision. An eerie silence fills the chamber as the heavy vault door swings open on silent hinges. But deep within the police surveillance center, automated alarms flicker to life on glowing computer monitors. Detective Vance grabs his trench coat and radios the pursuit units idling in the dark avenue. Squad cars tear through empty midnight streets with sirens echoing off glass skyscraper towers. Tire tracks lead towards an abandoned industrial shipping warehouse beside the foggy river docks. Flashing red and blue lights surround the rusted metal gates as tactical officers take perimeter positions in the pouring rain. Inside the warehouse, heavy wooden crates sit stacked beside a waiting black speed boat on the dark waters. Footsteps scramble across the iron catwalk above as the suspects attempt an escape across the wet rooftop. Cornered against the dark river skyline, the suspects surrender under blinding searchlight beams. The stolen centuries-old diamonds are secured safely inside evidence lockboxes as dawn breaks over the city skyline.
